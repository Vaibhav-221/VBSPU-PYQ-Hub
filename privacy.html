1. Overview & Scope

Welcome to VBSPU PYQ Hub (https://vbspu-pyq-hub.online). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, store, handle, and protect your personal information when you visit and use our website.

VBSPU PYQ Hub is an open educational platform designed to provide authentic previous year question papers (PYQs), syllabus materials, and study resources to students of Veer Bahadur Singh Purvanchal University (VBSPU). By accessing or using our website, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

4. Cookies & Web Beacons

Like any other website, VBSPU PYQ Hub uses 'cookies'. These cookies are used to store information including visitors' preferences, and the pages on the website that the visitor accessed or visited. The information is used to optimize the users' experience by customizing our web page content based on visitors' browser type and/or other information.

How to Control Cookies:

You can choose to disable cookies through your individual browser options. To know more detailed information about cookie management with specific web browsers, it can be found at the browsers' respective websites (e.g., Google Chrome, Mozilla Firefox, Microsoft Edge, Apple Safari).

We provide an optional opportunity for users to support the project via voluntary micro-donations (e.g., ₹19) to cover server, domain, and hosting costs. All monetary transactions are processed securely through Razorpay Payment Gateway using PCI-DSS compliant infrastructure.

Another priority for us is adding protection for children while using the internet. VBSPU PYQ Hub does not knowingly collect any Personal Identifiable Information from children under the age of 13.
